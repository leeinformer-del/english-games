Mission 4: Personal Speaking

Teacher asks. Encourage the student to answer in complete sentences.

1. What is your favorite color?
2. What is your favorite animal?
3. What color is your favorite animal?
4. What animal do you want to see?
5. Why do you like it?
Teacher Focus
Ask “Why?” or “Tell me more.” when the student gives a short answer.
